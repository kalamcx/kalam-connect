# PDISC-2 Decision Lock — Timeline / Social Graph

**Decision date:** 30/09/2026  
**Status:** LOCKED  
**Authority:** Kalam Connect product owner

## Locked decisions

- Timeline is member-driven social content; Magazine remains editorial and Messages remains private chat.
- Member social objects are Original Post, Magazine Share, Quote Post, Repost, Community-scoped Post, plus projections of official/tagged content.
- Repost is distribution-only with no separate reactions/comments.
- Quote Post is a new post with commentary and its own reactions/comments.
- Magazine Share keeps its own Timeline discussion while referencing the canonical Magazine publication.
- Member-post audience is initially either all members or an eligible Community.
- Timeline cannot be used for private small-group messaging.
- Sharing/reposting never widens source visibility.
- Published post destination/audience is immutable.
- Tag and Mention are different:
  - Tag creates profile/Timeline projection.
  - Mention links/notifies but does not create Tagged profile projection.
- Tagged members can hide their profile projection or remove their tag from member-authored posts.
- Official tag/recognition corrections remain publisher-controlled.
- Social graph is one-way Follow; there are no friend requests/connections.
- Initial feeds are For You, Following, Latest, Communities.
- For You ranking is understandable and deterministic; it does not use private messages or HR/performance data.
- Post reactions use Like, Love, Celebrate, Support, Wow, Angry.
- Comments/replies use a lightweight Like initially.
- Comment threading is one nested level.
- Member post edits retain revision evidence and show Edited.
- Post destination, kind, source and primary media are immutable after publish in MVP.
- Normal deletion is soft deletion.
- Block removes/prevents social interaction but does not erase required company identity or official content.
- Mute privately suppresses social content without blocking.
- Report volume alone does not automatically remove content.
- Staff social analytics are aggregate and may not become employee-performance scoring or viewer surveillance.
- Inactive members lose social actions but historical content remains subject to retention/moderation policy.
- Feed-ranking failure falls back to Latest chronological eligible content.
